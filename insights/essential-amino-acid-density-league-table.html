Summary: key findings

  • Per gram of protein, whey isolate wins, but by less than marketing implies. Total essential amino acids (the nine EAAs, summed) per gram of protein: whey protein isolate 516.3 mg/g, egg white powder 468.8 mg/g, whole dry milk 464.9 mg/g, WPC80 462.7 mg/g, WPC34 450.8 mg/g, whole dried egg 434.1 mg/g. The full spread from first to last is 19%: 516.3 / 434.1 = 1.19. Protein source matters; it is not a 2× decision on this axis.
  • Per 100 kcal, the ranking flips. Egg white powder delivers 10,274 mg EAA per 100 kcal and WPC80 delivers 8,985 mg, while whole dried egg falls to 3,625 mg and whole dry milk to 2,467 mg, because their fat carries calories that carry no amino acids. WPI still leads at 12,628 mg/100 kcal. A ranking built for a lean RTD and a ranking built for a meal replacement are different tables.
  • Per dollar, the cheaper whey grade wins. At publicly reported US spot prices on November 5, 2025 (Vesper price index, reporting USDA Dairy Market News market conditions): WPC80 at $6.35/lb delivers 26.44 g EAA per dollar; WPI at $10.70/lb delivers 19.86 g per dollar. WPC80 gives 33% more EAA per dollar: 26.44 / 19.86 = 1.33. Leucine per dollar is nearly tied (5.77 g vs 5.51 g), because WPI's leucine density partly offsets its price.
  • GAP, stated plainly: plant proteins are not in this table, and per-dollar data is whey-only. No public commodity price reporting for plant protein ingredients (soy, pea, rice isolates) comparable to USDA Dairy Market News for whey could be sourced on 2026-11-05, and no public bulk price for egg powders was sourced either. Per the program's integrity rules, those rankings are not estimated. The league table below is dairy + egg only, and the per-dollar ranking covers only the two whey grades with a verified public price. Closing this gap is a named task for the next cycle.

So what: Pick your ranking axis before you pick your ingredient. Per-gram, per-calorie, and per-dollar tables crown different winners from the same underlying profiles, and a formulator who optimizes the wrong axis overpays or under-delivers.

Why it matters: Ingredient buying is usually argued on price per pound or protein percentage. Neither is the unit your label claim or your customer's nutrition panel runs on. EAA density in the unit you actually sell (per gram of protein claimed, per calorie declared, per dollar spent) is.

Table 1: EAA density per gram of protein and per 100 kcal

Profiles: whey grades from the U.S. Dairy Export Council Reference Manual for U.S. Whey and Lactose Products (2005), p.51–52, which reproduces USDA nutrient database values; egg and milk profiles from USDA FoodData Central (SR Legacy). Accessed 2026-11-05. EAA total = His + Ile + Leu + Lys + Met + Phe + Thr + Trp + Val. Full worksheet: data.csv.

Whey protein isolate (WPI) 90.75 371 46.85 516.3 12,628 143.3
Egg white, dried, powder 82.4 376 38.63 468.8 10,274 87.0
WPC80 80.00 412 37.02 462.7 8,985 101.0
Milk, dry, whole 26.32 496 12.24 464.9 2,467 97.9
WPC34 34.36 369 15.49 450.8 4,198 102.4
Egg, whole, dried 48.1 576 20.88 434.1 3,625 84.1

Worked example, WPI per gram of protein: EAA sum = 1.35 + 5.90 + 13.00 + 9.15 + 2.05 + 2.30 + 6.25 + 1.50 + 5.35 = 46.85 g per 100 g. 46.85 g ÷ 90.75 g protein = 0.5163 g/g = 516.3 mg/g protein. Per 100 kcal: 46,850 mg ÷ 371 kcal × 100 = 12,628 mg/100 kcal.

Read the milk row carefully: whole dry milk ranks third per gram of protein (464.9 mg/g) and last per calorie (2,467 mg/100 kcal). Both numbers are correct. They answer different questions.

Table 2: EAA per dollar, whey grades only (verified public prices)

Prices: Vesper US price index, November 5, 2025, EXW: WPC80 instant $6.35/lb, WPI instant $10.70/lb, reported during a period of record whey tightness attributed to factory downtime. These are spot prices on one date, not contract prices. Accessed 2026-11-05.

Ingredient Price ($/lb) EAA (g/lb) EAA (g/$) Leucine (g/$)
WPC80 6.35 167.9 26.44 5.77
WPI 10.70 212.5 19.86 5.51

Worked example, WPC80: EAA per pound = 37.016 g/100 g × 4.53592 (100-g units per lb) = 167.9 g. 167.9 ÷ 6.35 = 26.44 g/$. Leucine: 8.08 × 4.53592 = 36.65 g/lb; 36.65 ÷ 6.35 = 5.77 g/$.

USDA Dairy Market News also reported dry whey at $0.71/lb (CME weekly average, week ending November 7, 2025, Mideast Market Administrator bulletin). Dry whey is not WPC34 (it is lower in protein), so it is not entered into this table as a WPC34 price. It is recorded here so the next cycle can add a dry-whey row with its own matching profile.

Perspectives

Formulator. For label targets (grams of protein, EAA claims), Table 1's per-gram column is your axis. WPI's leucine density (143.3 mg/g protein) is in a class of its own; the next best is WPC34 at 102.4.

Procurement. Per-dollar, the premium grade is paying for concentration and leucine, not for total EAAs. If your constraint is EAA grams per dollar, WPC80 wins at November 2025 spot prices. If your constraint is leucine per serving in a small format, rerun the math on the leucine column before concluding.

Product. For calorie-counted formats (RTDs, shots), the per-100-kcal column is the honest one, and fat-bearing ingredients (whole egg, whole milk) fall sharply. Egg white powder is the quiet winner of that table after WPI.

Skeptic. Density is not quality. This table does not adjust for digestibility or limiting amino acids; a density leader can still be limiting in one EAA. That adjustment is Topic 3's job, and this table should not be quoted without that caveat.

Call to action

Verify the EAA density of the ingredient lot you are actually buying, not the book profile: Total Amino Acid Analysis, $230/sample. Supplier profiles are typical values; your lot is a specific value.

Methods & Sources

  • EAA definition: the nine essential amino acids (His, Ile, Leu, Lys, Met, Phe, Thr, Trp, Val). Cysteine and tyrosine are excluded from the EAA sum (they are conditionally essential sparing partners, reported in data.csv source profiles where available).
  • Whey profiles: USDEC Reference Manual for U.S. Whey and Lactose Products (2005), p.51–52, table of selected commercial whey products (dry sweet whey, WPC34, WPC80, WPI). Values may vary by manufacturer, per the source itself.
  • Egg white powder, whole dried egg, whole dry milk profiles: USDA FoodData Central, SR Legacy entries, read via public mirrors on 2026-11-05 (see calc-notes.md). Whole dry milk amino acids were converted from the published per-cup (128 g) values by dividing by 1.28.
  • Prices: Vesper news report of its US price index, November 5, 2025 (WPC80 $6.35/lb, WPI $10.70/lb, EXW). USDA Dairy Market News dry whey $0.71/lb via Mideast Market Administrator's Bulletin, November 2025.
  • GATE OUTCOME: plant protein prices could not be publicly sourced on 2026-11-05, so no plant ingredient is ranked in this version, and egg ingredients carry no per-dollar figure. This is a data gap, reported as a finding, not filled by estimation.
