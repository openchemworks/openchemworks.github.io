Amino Acid Analysis by AQC Derivatization and HPLC

19 amino acids and ammonia per sample, an extended panel on request, and a report that includes the chromatogram and the QC behind every number.

Price & bookExample report (PDF)

Free amino acids, reported in µM and mg/L

The standard profile (AA-1) measures 19 amino acids and ammonia by pre-column derivatization with AQC, reversed-phase HPLC on a C18 column and UV detection at 260 nm. The extended panel (AA-2) adds five non-protein amino acids to the same run. Clarification and deproteinization of liquid samples are included.

How the chemistry works is in the methods library; how we control quality is on its own page; how to read a result is in reading an amino acid profile.

Total amino acids and creatine

For the amino acid composition of proteins, powders and hydrolysates, see total amino acid analysis (acid hydrolysis, $230 per sample). For creatine products, see creatine testing ($120 per sample).

Analytes reported

Standard panel (AA-1), 19 amino acids + ammonia: alanine, arginine, asparagine, aspartate, glutamate, glutamine, glycine, histidine, isoleucine, leucine, lysine, methionine, phenylalanine, proline, serine, threonine, tryptophan, tyrosine, valine; ammonia.

Extended panel (AA-2): GABA, ornithine, citrulline, hydroxyproline — on request. Taurine is measured on request.

AA-3 (cysteine / cystine, reduction–alkylation workup): on request.
