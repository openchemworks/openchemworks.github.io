Short answer

At our published prices, supplement testing for amino acids and creatine costs $120 to $230 per sample. Free amino acids (19 amino acids plus ammonia) cost $150 per sample, or $125 at 15 or more a month and $99 at 30 or more a month on a 6-month plan. Total amino acids cost $230, creatine in powders, capsules and drinks costs $120, and creatine in gummies and chews costs $180 (includes the matrix spike-recovery check). The minimum order is $180, and new customers can start with a five-sample pilot for $250. Every report includes the chromatogram.

The numbers, up front

Pricing behind a sales process means a discovery call before a number and a week gone before you know whether testing is in budget. Many brands run pre-compliance screening at $150 to $180 per sample to catch formulation gaps or degradation early, before paying for a formal accredited test. See choosing an amino acid analysis laboratory, creatine gummy testing cost and degradation, how to verify amino acids on a supplement label, and the amino acid testing price index.
