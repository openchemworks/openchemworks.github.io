Why pairing 15 uL HPLC with ratio QC saves multi-site cohorts

When animal bleeds or research draws sit at room temperature past 2 hours before centrifugation, red blood cells and platelets continue converting glutamine into glutamate, shifting Glu/Gln sharply upward (full literature synthesis). Flagging delayed-spin outliers before running multivariate statistics prevents bench handling drift from masquerading as treatment biology.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What ratios does this plasma amino acid calculator compute?

It computes Fischer's Ratio ((Val + Ile + Leu) / (Phe + Tyr)), Global Arginine Bioavailability Ratio (Arg / (Orn + Cit)), Phe / Tyr, Total BCAA (uM), and the Glu / Gln pre-analytical stability ratio.

Why screen plasma cohorts for Glu/Gln ratio elevation?

Unseparated whole blood held at room temperature for 2 hours undergoes ongoing enzymatic glutamine deamidation to glutamate (Kamlage et al. 2014). Screening cohorts for elevated Glu/Gln catches tube-handling delays before statistical modeling mistakes storage artifacts for biology.

Is this tool intended for clinical diagnosis?

No. Both this browser tool and OpenChemWorks' 15 uL plasma HPLC service ($150/sample AA-1, RUO) are strictly for research and pre-analytical quality-control exploration, not clinical diagnosis or patient care.
